Privacy Policy

§1

Overview

Circuit Builder (the "App") is developed by Clover Studio ("we", "us"). It teaches electronics fundamentals through breadboard puzzles, and has no server, no user account, and no internet connection of its own. Everything about your progress lives in one file on your device.

This policy covers the App as distributed on Google Play. It does not cover Google Play itself, which has its own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

§2

Data stored on your device

The App keeps a single local save file, written to its own private storage area on your device and never transmitted anywhere:

FieldWhat it is
Solved levelsWhich puzzles you've completed, so the game can resume where you left off.
Full-game unlock statusWhether the one-time purchase has been made (see §4).
Audio & haptics settingsYour music/sound-effect volume and whether vibration feedback is on.

None of this identifies you personally, and none of it leaves your device under the App's own control.

§3

What we don't collect

The App does not include analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising software of any kind. Specifically, it does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any other personal identifier
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers used for advertising or tracking
  • Usage analytics or telemetry of any kind
  • Photos, contacts, or any other data on your device
The App requests no permissions beyond what Android grants automatically to run. It cannot read anything else on your device, and it does not ask you to create an account.
§4

Purchases & Google Play Billing

Circuit Builder is free to download, with one optional one-time purchase that unlocks every chapter. That purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing: Google, not the developer, collects and processes your payment details.

When a purchase completes, the App receives only a purchase confirmation token from Google Play, enough to know the unlock should apply, and nothing about your payment method, card details, or billing address. See Google's own privacy policy for how they handle payment information.

§5

Data sharing

We don't share, sell, or transmit any data, because the App doesn't collect any to begin with. The only outside party involved at all is Google Play, for the purchase flow described in §4.

§6

Children's privacy

Because the App collects no personal data from anyone, of any age, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The educational content is suitable for a general audience.

§7

Your choices

You're always in control of the one file the App keeps:

  • Reset progress: the Settings screen's "Reset Progress" button clears your solved-level history on this device.
  • Delete everything: uninstalling the App removes its save file along with it. There is no server copy to delete separately, because none exists.
§8

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change with it. Since the App has no way to notify you directly, check back here occasionally if you'd like to stay current.

§9

Contact

Questions about this policy or the App's data handling can be sent to playcloverstudio@gmail.com.
